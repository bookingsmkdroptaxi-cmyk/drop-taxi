About MK Drop Taxi

Tamil Nadu's trusted one-way outstation taxi service provider headquartered in Krishnagiri

Our Story

Headquartered in Pochampalli, Krishnagiri, MK Drop Taxi was established with a singular mission: eliminating the unfair practice of charging passengers return fares for one-way journeys. We recognized that thousands of commuters traveling between Krishnagiri, Hosur, Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, and Bangalore were paying double for outstation cabs. Today, MK Drop Taxi operates a network of over 500+ verified vehicles connecting all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu and neighboring states.

Our Fleet & Service Coverage

From compact Sedans for solo business trips to spacious Innovas and Tempo Travellers for family pilgrimages and vacation tours, our fleet is fully air-conditioned, regularly serviced, and thoroughly sanitized. We serve all major airports (Chennai, Bangalore, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy) and railway junctions 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Years of Experience & Safety First

  • Verified Drivers: Every driver undergoes background checks, route orientation, and safety training.
  • GPS Tracking: Real-time satellite tracking on every active booking for complete peace of mind.
  • Transparent Billing: No hidden costs, no peak surge pricing, and no surprise waiting fees.

Privacy Policy — MK Drop Taxi

Last updated: September 8, 2026

MK Drop Taxi ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and booking service. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide: Name, phone number, email address.
  • Trip details: Pickup and drop locations, travel date, pickup time, cab type.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage your taxi booking.
  • To contact you regarding pickup details, driver assignment, and trip updates.
  • To calculate distance and fare using your pickup/drop locations.
  • To improve our website and customer service experience.
  • To send booking confirmations, receipts, and optional promotional offers.

3. Location Data

Pickup and drop addresses you enter are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoding service and OSRM routing service solely to calculate distance, travel time, and display the route on a map. We do not use this data for any purpose beyond fare calculation and trip planning.

4. Sharing of Information

  • We share only necessary trip details (pickup/drop address, scheduled time, passenger name, phone number) with the driver assigned to your trip.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We may disclose information if required by Indian law or court orders.

5. Data Retention

Booking records are retained for up to 2 years for accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance, after which they are securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at bookings.mkdroptaxi@gmail.com or +91 63799 15890.

7. Cookies

We use cookies/local storage only to remember your recent route search for convenience. You can clear these at any time via your browser settings.

Terms & Conditions, Cancellation and Refund Policy — MK Drop Taxi

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Booking Confirmation

  • A booking is confirmed only after you receive a confirmation call/SMS/WhatsApp message from MK Drop Taxi with driver and vehicle details.
  • Fare shown on the website at the time of booking is an estimate based on OSRM calculated distance; toll fees, parking charges, interstate entry permits, and hill station charges (if applicable) are extra and charged on actuals.

2. Advance Payment

An advance payment of 20% may be collected to confirm high-demand holiday or peak-season trips. The balance amount is payable directly to the driver at the end of the trip via cash or UPI.

3. Cancellation by Customer

  • Free Cancellation: Made at least 3 hours before the scheduled pickup time.
  • Late Cancellation: Cancellations made within 3 hours of pickup, or after a driver has already dispatched to the pickup point, may attract a flat cancellation fee of ₹300 to cover driver allocation costs.
  • No-Shows: If the customer is unreachable or not present at the pickup location within 30 minutes of the scheduled time, the booking will be treated as a no-show and any advance paid shall be non-refundable.

4. Cancellation by MK Drop Taxi

In the rare event we are unable to provide a cab (vehicle breakdown, extreme weather, driver emergency), we will notify you immediately and either arrange an alternate cab or issue a 100% full refund of any advance paid.

5. Refunds

  • Eligible refunds are processed to the original payment method / UPI within 5–7 business days.
  • No refund is provided once the trip has commenced, except in cases of proven service failure attributable to MK Drop Taxi.

6. Fare & Distance Disputes

Distance is calculated using OpenStreetMap-based routing at the time of booking. Actual vehicle odometer distance may vary slightly due to traffic diversions or customer-requested route changes; any material difference will be settled fairly with supporting trip log data.

7. Passenger Conduct & Luggage

  • Standard luggage allowance per cab type applies. Excess luggage requiring a larger vehicle will be charged accordingly.
  • Smoking, alcohol consumption, and carriage of illegal substances are strictly prohibited inside all cabs.

8. Limitation of Liability

MK Drop Taxi is not liable for delays caused by heavy traffic, weather conditions, natural disasters, road closures, or other force majeure events beyond our control.

9. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India and subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu.
